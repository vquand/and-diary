# and-diary

A planned handwritten diary for Android tablets with stylus support. Open a date from the monthly calendar, write on a continuous sheet with customizable paper sections, and paste, move, or resize photos alongside your writing. Entries autosave locally and support manual backup and restore.

The plan covers the S Pen prototype and build order. [Implementation plan](PLAN.md)
